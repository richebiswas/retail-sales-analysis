# 📊 Retail Sales Analysis

## 📌 Overview

A Python-based retail sales analysis project focused on identifying sales trends, customer and product performance, regional differences, and seasonal patterns from transactional data.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and order performance
- Identify top-performing categories and products
- Analyze customer and sales concentration
- Compare regional performance
- Identify monthly and yearly sales trends
- Generate actionable business insights

## 🛠️ Tools

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook

## 📈 Key Findings

- Technology is the strongest-performing category.
- West is the highest-performing region.
- South contributes approximately 17.2% of total sales.
- Top 10 customers contribute approximately 6.8% of total sales.
- 168 customers account for 50% of cumulative sales.
- Top 10 products contribute approximately 10.8% of total sales.
- November shows the strongest average monthly sales performance.
- November 2018 showed unusually strong sales and requires further investigation.
